Lyman-α transmission model

I have designed a flexible code in Python that models the Lyman-α damping wing transmission through the intergalactic medium during the Epoch of Reionization. This is highly user-friendly and can be easily adapted to different reionization simulations. All it needs is a halo catalog and the hydrogen ionization field from the simulation. Please feel free to use it and provide feedback, as I continue to improve it.
