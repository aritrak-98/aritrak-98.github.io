About me

Bio

Welcome! I am a PhD student at the University of Pennsylvania, working on near-field cosmology and reionization.

I use Feedback In Realistic Environments (FIRE) zoomed-in cosmological simulations to study the Epoch of Reionization through near-field cosmology. I also model Lyman-alpha emission from galaxies in large-scale simulations like THESAN.

I aim to use my expertise to understand the role of different radiative feedbacks.
